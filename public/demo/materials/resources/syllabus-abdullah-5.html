Artificial Intelligence — Course Syllabus

Course: Applied Mathematics · Subject: Artificial Intelligence · Semester: Semester 5 · Year: 2026-2027

Course scope

Separate a problem’s state representation, available actions, goal condition, and cost. Compare search strategies and evaluation measures, and record what assumptions the model makes about its data.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the core terminology and assumptions used in Artificial Intelligence.
  • Apply the relevant methods to a worked problem and justify the chosen approach.
  • Interpret results, identify limitations, and communicate a clear conclusion.

Suggested sequence

  1. Foundations and vocabulary
  2. Worked examples and guided practice
  3. Applications, review, and an integrative problem
