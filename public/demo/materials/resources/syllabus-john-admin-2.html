Applied Mathematics — Course Syllabus

Course: Computer Science · Subject: Applied Mathematics · Semester: Semester 2 · Year: 2025-2026

Course scope

Write the model and assumptions before simplifying. Keep dimensions consistent through each transformation and verify a result with a boundary value or an independent method.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the core terminology and assumptions used in Applied Mathematics.
  • Apply the relevant methods to a worked problem and justify the chosen approach.
  • Interpret results, identify limitations, and communicate a clear conclusion.

Suggested sequence

  1. Foundations and vocabulary
  2. Worked examples and guided practice
  3. Applications, review, and an integrative problem
