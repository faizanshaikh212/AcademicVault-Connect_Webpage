Computer Science — Course Syllabus

Course: Computer Science · Subject: Computer Science · Semester: Semester 1 · Year: 2025-2026

Course scope

Connect programming fundamentals, data representation, algorithms, and system behavior. Explain how a design choice affects correctness, complexity, and the person using the software.

Learning outcomes

  • Explain the core terminology and assumptions used in Computer Science.
  • Apply the relevant methods to a worked problem and justify the chosen approach.
  • Interpret results, identify limitations, and communicate a clear conclusion.

Suggested sequence

  1. Foundations and vocabulary
  2. Worked examples and guided practice
  3. Applications, review, and an integrative problem
