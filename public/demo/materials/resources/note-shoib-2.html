Software Engineering — Study Notes

Course: Applied Mathematics · Subject: Software Engineering · Semester: Semester 2 · Year: 2026-2027

Core ideas

Translate requirements into observable acceptance criteria, then connect design, implementation, testing, and maintenance. Keep decisions and risks explicit so a reviewer can understand why a change was made.

Check your understanding

  1. Define the main terms in your own words and explain how they connect.
  2. Work through a small example, showing each assumption and intermediate step.
  3. Describe one common mistake and the check that would catch it.

Revision checklist

  • Summarize the topic without copying the source paragraph.
  • Mark any point that still needs confirmation in class material.
  • Revisit the worked example after a short break.
